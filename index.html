About

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the College of Computing and Mathematical Sciences, Khalifa University of Science and Technology. My research lies at the interface of spatial statistics, time series, functional data analysis and stochastic processes.

I am interested in methods that are both mathematically principled and useful for real data, particularly in environmental, economic and health applications. I came to mathematics from a background in actuarial science.

Research

Stochastic processes and space–time models

Probabilistic models for random phenomena evolving over space and time, from random fields and spatio-temporal models to time series and Markov or latent-state structures, together with their estimation, inference and prediction.

Functional data analysis

Methods for data observed as curves, surfaces or other functions, including representation, dimension reduction and clustering.

Applications in environment, economics and health

Statistical analysis of environmental, economic and health data, where dependence across space, time and individuals shapes both the questions and the methods.
